Your Moon mission 🌕

Keep your crew safe until sunrise. The Moon gets dark for a long time—save power now!

Read the full mission briefing

Your crew lands near the Moon's equator at local sunrise. Over six five-day turns, you must conserve resources and protect the habitat. Sunlight powers your systems early; the long lunar night is much harder.

Mission success

Finish turn six with Crew health at least 5 and Base at least 5.
