The First Night • Mission 01

Can you survive the lunar night?

Take charge of a two-person Moon outpost. Choose a crew, manage limited oxygen, water and power, and make six mission decisions before sunrise. Your choices matter—even when luck doesn't cooperate.

Approx. 5–8 minutes • Ages 7–12
Mission briefing

One lunar day. One long night.

Your crew lands near the Moon's equator at local sunrise. Over six five-day turns, you must conserve resources and protect the habitat. Sunlight powers your systems early; the long lunar night is much harder.

Mission success

Finish turn six with Crew at least 5 and Base at least 5.
